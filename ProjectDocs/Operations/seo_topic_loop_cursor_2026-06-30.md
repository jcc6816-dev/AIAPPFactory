## 6. Entry and exit gates

### To enter SERP capture

A topic must have at least one of:

- direct MVP product support,
- existing asset overlap that could be tuned later,
- clear relation to an active cluster after its freeze window,
- user/business priority strong enough to justify manual SERP work.

### To enter Architect

A topic must have all of:

- real Google US SERP evidence with screenshots or saved HTML,
- a Codex product-truth review,
- clear owner asset decision: existing page tune, template-only, post-only, or new asset,
- no conflict with an active freeze window,
- no MVP capability overclaim.

### To enter Build

A topic must have all Architect gates plus:

- explicit build brief,
- file/page ownership,
- CTA path and intent tracking plan,
- SEO/schema/canonical plan,
- verification steps,
- no more than one Active Build at a time.

## Product truth fit for GenForms.ai

**GenForms MVP capabilities that match**:
- Native webhook support (generic / Feishu / DingTalk / WeCom / Slack providers) without requiring Apps Script.
- AI-native form generation and conversational form experience.
- Lower pricing story vs Typeform ($19/mo Pro vs Typeform Basic $29/mo; Typeform webhooks require $89+/mo plans).
- FormSkill ecosystem includes data cleaning, OCR, AI insights, email notification, and report export — features Google Forms lacks.

**GenForms MVP limitations that are gaps**:
- Brand authority is far below Tally, Fillout, Jotform, and Formaloo; Google AI Overview does not mention GenForms.
- No direct Zapier/Make native integration yet; webhooks are generic endpoint only.
- Organic domain authority is low compared to established competitors and high-DR listicle sites.

**Overall fit**: Strong
**Matching count**: 3 (native webhooks, AI-native, price advantage)
**Gap count**: 2 (brand authority, native integrations)

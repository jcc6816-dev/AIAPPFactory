## Codex intent and product-truth decision

The cluster is broad and highly commercial. Searchers expect responsive drag-and-drop builders and often also native mobile apps, offline collection, mobile-side form creation, e-signatures, kiosk/field workflows or deep customization.

GenForms has a genuine mobile-friendly public filling flow, share links and QR access, but it does not currently offer a visual drag-and-drop editor, native mobile app, offline field collection or the broader mobile operations suite implied by much of this SERP.

Decision: `Reject standalone Topic / Merge into product experience`.

Do not create a mobile-form-builder landing page. Keep “mobile-friendly” as supporting copy on task-specific pages where the capability is visible and truthful.

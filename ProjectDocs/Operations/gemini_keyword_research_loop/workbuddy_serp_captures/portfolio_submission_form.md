## Codex intent and ownership decision

The cluster mixes two real intents:

1. template/form intent for collecting creator details, portfolio links or uploads, descriptions and permissions;
2. admissions/how-to intent for preparing and submitting art-school portfolios.

GenForms already has both owning assets and real product support:

- `/templates/portfolio-submission`
- `/solutions/portfolio-submission-form-template`
- the Template includes portfolio URL/file fields, and the submission route handles uploaded files.

The exact template query already shows the existing GenForms Solution on page one. Creating another URL would split ownership.

Decision: `Existing asset / Observe / Golden Tuning candidate`.

Do not create a new page. Confirm the same query-to-page relationship in GSC before changing title, FAQ, fields or internal links.

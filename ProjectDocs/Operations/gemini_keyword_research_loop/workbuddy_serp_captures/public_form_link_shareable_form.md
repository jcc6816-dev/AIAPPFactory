## Codex intent and ownership decision

The cluster splits into two intents:

1. `shareable form link` and `public form link` are beginner help queries dominated by Google/Microsoft/Jotform support content.
2. `create shareable online form` is a broad category/product-discovery query dominated by large free form builders.

GenForms genuinely supports public links, QR sharing and mobile form filling, but these are baseline capabilities rather than a defensible standalone Topic. A dedicated page would overlap the homepage, template pages and existing QR Form assets without adding enough independent value.

Decision: `Merge / Product education and existing QR-sharing assets / No new page`.

Keep public-link language as supporting product copy and onboarding/help material. Do not create a standalone SEO landing page unless future GSC evidence identifies a narrower query with a clear owning asset.

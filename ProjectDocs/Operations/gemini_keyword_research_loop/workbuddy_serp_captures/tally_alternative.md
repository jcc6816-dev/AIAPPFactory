**Secondary intent**: Compare free vs paid alternatives, read reviews, and evaluate feature trade-offs.

**Commercial intent**: High. Searchers are comparison shopping and evaluating named alternatives.

**User profile**: Founders, product managers, no-code builders, and ops teams who have outgrown Tally or want specific capabilities (design, integrations, AI, webhooks).

## Product truth fit for GenForms.ai

**GenForms MVP capabilities that match**:
- AI-native form generation (Tally has AI but GenForms is built around conversational AI form creation).
- Native webhooks without workarounds (Tally requires paid plan or integrations for advanced webhooks).
- Lower paid-tier pricing story vs Typeform and potentially comparable to Tally Business/Pro tiers.
- FormSkill ecosystem (OCR, data cleaning, AI insights, report export) adds differentiation beyond simple forms.

**GenForms MVP limitations that are gaps**:
- Tally's free tier is extremely generous; GenForms cannot compete on "free" alone.
- Brand/domain authority far below Tally, Fillout, Typeform, Jotform, G2, and Zapier.
- Google AI Overview does not mention GenForms as an alternative.
- No direct integration ecosystem comparable to Tally's many embed/automation options.

**Overall fit**: Moderate-Strong
**Matching count**: 3 (AI-native, webhooks, unique FormSkills)
**Gap count**: 2 (free-tier value perception, brand authority)

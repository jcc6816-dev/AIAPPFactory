## Product truth fit for GenForms.ai

**GenForms MVP capabilities that match**:
- **Already ranking** for `user interview recruitment form` with a dedicated template page at genforms.ai/templates/user-interview-recruitment-form.
- AI-native form generation can quickly produce screener questions tailored to specific participant criteria.
- Native webhooks can push qualified participants to calendars, CRMs, email tools, or databases.
- FormSkills (data cleaning, email notification, AI insights) can automate follow-up and qualification scoring.
- Lower cost than dedicated UX research recruitment platforms (User Interviews, Respondent).

**GenForms MVP limitations that are gaps**:
- GenForms does not provide a participant panel (User Interviews, Respondent, and Respondent.io do).
- No native calendar scheduling integration yet; users would need webhooks + Calendly/Cal.com.
- Brand authority in UX research space is far below User Interviews and Nielsen Norman Group.

**Overall fit**: Strong

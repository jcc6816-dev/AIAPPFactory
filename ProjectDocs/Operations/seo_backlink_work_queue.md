## 1. 外链工作的目标

外链不是为了“堆数量”，而是为了给 Google 和真实用户传递这些信号：

1. GenForms.ai 是真实存在、被外部页面提到的工具。
2. GenForms.ai 和 `AI form builder`、`Typeform alternative`、`form builder with webhook`、`contact form builder`、`QR code form builder` 等主题有关。
3. 外部用户或目录把我们放在正确语境中，而不是随便一个低质站点挂链接。
4. 外部流量进入后能被 GA4 / GSC / 后台增长事件识别。

这条线和页面 SEO 的关系：

- 页面 SEO：让 Google 理解“我们应该展示给谁”。
- 外链/品牌信号：让 Google 更愿意相信“我们值得排到更前面”。

## 2. 工作原则

### 2.1 允许

- 真实工具目录。
- SaaS / no-code / AI / form builder / workflow automation 相关目录。
- AlternativeTo、SaaSHub、BetaList、Uneed、Fazier、DevHunt、Tiny Startups 等真实产品目录。
- Reddit、Indie Hackers、HN、X、LinkedIn 中有帮助的经验分享或问题回答。
- 博客、教程、资源列表、工具合集中的真实推荐。
- 使用 UTM 做归因。
- 明确说明自己是 GenForms.ai 创建者或团队成员。

### 2.2 禁止

- 购买传递排名权重的链接。
- 批量提交低质目录。
- 自动生成评论外链。
- 假装成普通用户推荐 GenForms。
- 在无关帖子里硬贴链接。
- 使用夸大宣传，例如 `best`, `#1`, `guaranteed`, `unlimited free`, `100% Typeform replacement`。
- 一天提交几十个外链，导致信号异常且无法归因。

### 6.3 社区回复模板

#### Typeform pricing / alternative

```text
I would separate the decision into two questions:

1. Do you need Typeform's mature survey ecosystem and brand polish?
2. Or do you mainly need a focused form that is fast to launch and easy to connect to follow-up workflows?

If it is the second case, a lighter Typeform-style builder can be enough. Full disclosure: I am building GenForms.ai, so I am biased, but our angle is AI-generated forms + one-question-at-a-time flow + webhook-ready follow-up rather than trying to copy every Typeform feature.

This comparison may help if you are evaluating that route:
{URL}
```

#### Webhook form / delivery logs

```text
For webhook forms, I would not only ask "does it support webhooks?" but also "can I see what happened when delivery fails?"

The painful cases are usually 4xx/5xx responses, timeouts, payload mismatch, or auth mistakes. If the form tool does not show delivery status or retry visibility, the team may not notice failed handoff until much later.

I am working on GenForms.ai, where this is one of the workflow angles we care about. This page explains the approach:
{URL}
```

#### Website contact form

```text
For a simple website contact form, I would keep the first version very focused:

- name
- email
- company or website
- inquiry type
- message
- preferred response time

The bigger question is where responses go after submission: dashboard, CSV export, team notification, or webhook follow-up.

Full disclosure: I am building GenForms.ai. This page shows the website contact form angle we are using:
{URL}
```

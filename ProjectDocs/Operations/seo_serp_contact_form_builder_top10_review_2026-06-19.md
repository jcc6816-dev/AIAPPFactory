## 4. What Changed Versus The Earlier Conclusion

Earlier conclusion:

- Jotform and HubSpot were selected as the two main competitor samples.
- Rationale: Jotform represented templates; HubSpot represented post-submit business follow-up.

Revised conclusion after saved SERP sample:

- Jotform remains important, but it is not enough to define the market alone.
- HubSpot does not appear in this saved Top 10 sample, so it should not be treated as a primary `contact form builder` competitor for this keyword.
- The visible competitive set is more contact-specific and website-specific than the earlier conclusion suggested.
- The new primary sample set is: Shopify app listing, Jotform, POWR, Zoho Forms, AidaForm, Visme, Basin, 123FormBuilder, HelpDesk, Wufoo.

This changes the research interpretation:

- The market intent is more “give me a working website contact form now” than “teach me broad lead generation.”
- Website embed, no-code setup, templates, spam protection, email notification, and response viewing appear more central than CRM-style lead nurturing.
- Workflow still matters, but usually as notifications, integrations, spam protection, support/ticketing, or handoff rather than full CRM automation.

## 5. Search Intent Reconstructed From The Top 10

The saved Top 10 indicates that `contact form builder` has five strong intent layers:

1. Immediate creation: build a form now with no code.
2. Website publishing: embed on a website, page, Shopify store, or static site.
3. Template/field help: start from prebuilt contact-us fields or customize fields quickly.
4. Submission handling: view responses, receive email notifications, reduce spam, and hand off requests.
5. Business context: use contact forms for leads, customer messages, support, inquiries, or store operations.

Compared with the earlier Brief, the strongest missing dimension is not only “workflow”; it is “website publishing + spam/notification trust.”

## 9. Product Reuse Notes

This saved Top 10 is useful beyond SEO copy:

- Product onboarding: contact form users expect no-code setup, templates, response viewing, and publish/embed clarity.
- Template design: fields should include name, email/phone, company, inquiry type, message, consent or urgency.
- Future features: spam protection and email notification language appears repeatedly in the market; if not strong today, it is a product opportunity.
- Distribution: Shopify marketplace results show that app ecosystems may be a later acquisition channel.
- Trust: ratings/reviews and mature brand signals appear in the competitive environment; GenForms needs its own trust assets over time.

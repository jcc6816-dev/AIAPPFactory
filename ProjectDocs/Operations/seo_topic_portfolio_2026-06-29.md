## 1. Portfolio Rules

- Topic comes from repeated competitor/template/use-case evidence plus GenForms product fit. Keyword data validates and calibrates the Topic; it does not invent the Topic.
- Active Build is limited to one Topic. Active Research is limited to three Topics. Observe can run in parallel when every asset has a freeze date and named metrics.
- A SERP report is not permission to build. Every Topic must pass `Discover -> Validate -> Architect` and the product-truth gate first.
- No page may promise iframe/embed, production email, spam protection, native CRM sync, payment, ticketing, scheduling, approval, e-signature or unlimited free unless the product actually supports it.

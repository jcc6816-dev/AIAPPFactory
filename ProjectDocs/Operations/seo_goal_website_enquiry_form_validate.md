## Product Truth

Current GenForms can support a lightweight enquiry form with name, email/phone, company, enquiry type, message, preferred follow-up and consent to manual contact. It cannot promise inbox/helpdesk management, automatic routing, SLA, live chat, native CRM sync, spam protection, embed code or production email automation.

## Required Decision

Research whether Google US treats website/business enquiry intent as:

1. a direct synonym owned by the existing Contact Form pillar;
2. a Template intent that should be owned by `contact-us` or a renamed/new Template;
3. a regional-language variant that needs copy/internal-link coverage but no new URL;
4. a distinct product workflow that GenForms cannot currently complete.

Good to know.

Is there a free plan?+

Yes. Free forever, no credit card. You can build, publish and start collecting in under a minute. The Free plan is the trial; upgrade only when you outgrow it.

What's a credit, and what uses them?+

Credits are the fuel for automated work: running a workflow, looking up a company or person, or an AI agent doing a task. Building forms and tables (and collecting form responses) never costs a credit.

Do form responses have limits?+

No. Forms and responses are unlimited on every plan, including Free. There are no per-response fees, ever.

What happens if I run low on credits?+

Your forms keep collecting responses. That's always free and unlimited. To keep running the credit-powered steps (workflows, look-ups, AI agents), move up a plan and the extra credits apply right away. Pick the plan whose monthly credits fit your volume, and move up anytime if you need more.

Monthly or annual?+

Either. Annual billing saves you up to about 16% versus paying month to month.

Do I pay per seat?+

There's no per-seat tax on collecting data: anyone can submit your forms for free. Paid plans include a bundle of team seats: 3 on Basic, 10 on Professional, 50 on Agency.

Is my data mine?+

Yes. Everything lands in your own TinyTables: records you own, not data parked in a tool you only rent.

Can I change or cancel anytime?+

Yes. Move between plans or cancel whenever you like, right from your account.

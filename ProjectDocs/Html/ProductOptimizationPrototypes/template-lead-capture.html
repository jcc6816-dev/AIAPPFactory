What does it collect?

Email, company, team size, use case, urgency, and preferred follow-up channel.

Where does data go?

Webhook endpoint, sales Slack channel, Feishu group, or a lightweight CRM pipeline.

Field strategy

Ask fewer questions, but make every answer useful for follow-up.

This page should not be a thin template shell. It should explain why each field exists and how the resulting submission supports sales or support workflows.

  • Work email identifies the lead and company domain.
  • Team size helps prioritize high-intent accounts.
  • Use case turns a raw form submission into an actionable sales note.
  • Webhook destination makes the template operational, not just visual.

Professional field defaults

Start with the fields most website contact forms actually need.

AI can customize the wording, but the default structure should feel familiar and complete.

  • Name, email or phone, and company or website.
  • Inquiry type, such as sales, support, partnership, or general question.
  • Message, budget or urgency, and preferred response time.
  • Optional consent field when the page needs an explicit permission note.

Submission handling

After someone submits, the creator needs a visible next step.

The ordinary contact form promise is not automation. It is confidence that the message arrived and can be followed up.

  • Dashboard list for replies and inquiry status.
  • CSV export for small teams that need a simple handoff.
  • QR code and public share link for website and offline entry points.
  • Webhook follow-up for advanced users who need routing.
Advanced differentiation

Webhook is the follow-up layer, not the headline.

For this page, Webhook should appear after the user understands the basic website contact form flow. The promise is: start simple, then route submissions to Feishu, DingTalk, WeCom, Slack Bot, or a generic webhook when the workflow needs it.
